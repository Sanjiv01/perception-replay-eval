# UI/UX gaps hit while building this pipeline, framed as backlog items

Each item names the moment it hurt, who it hurts, and what "done" looks like. Ordered by how much
time it cost during this build. P0 = blocked or misled me, P1 = slowed me down, P2 = polish.

| # | Pri | Backlog item | Where it hurt | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P0 | **Click a metric, land on the frame.** Metric tables and the viewer are disconnected. | Tracing the precision discrepancy went Parquet query → frame `timestamp_ns` → seek the MCAP to that time by hand. I had to write `replay trace` just to print the seek time. | Every number in an eval report is a link: slice → list of frames → opens the MCAP at that log time with the right topics enabled. |
| 2 | P0 | **Eval status as a first-class layer.** Boxes have no notion of TP/FP/FN, so overlays are just "two colours of cubes". | I had to bake status into colours and `metadata` at export time. Changing the match threshold means re-exporting every MCAP. | The viewer reads `matches.parquet` (or an equivalent topic) and colours by status live, with a threshold slider. |
| 3 | P0 | **Version A/B in one view.** Comparing two model versions means toggling `/pred/v1` and `/pred/v2` on and off. | Spotting "lost" GT (detected by v1, missed by v2) by eye is unreliable. | Split or diff mode: show only boxes whose status differs between versions, with a per-frame flip count on the timeline. |
| 4 | P1 | **Timeline heat strip.** No way to see where in a 15 s log the errors are. | The worst frames only came out of `replay trace`, never out of the viewer. | A per-frame sparkline of FP/FN (or any per-frame metric column) under the playback bar. Click to seek. |
| 5 | P1 | **Visible progress for distributed recompute.** Ray on Windows with the dashboard off shows only `:task_name:run_scene` in worker logs. | GPU at 0% for minutes looked like a hang. It was workers loading 150 MB of lidar each. | Per-task stage + frames/s reported back to the driver. The CLI shows a live per-scene bar. |
| 6 | P1 | **Crash-safe, resumable recompute by default.** The first job held all results in memory until the end. | A host crash (GPU driver TDR) threw away every finished scene. | Results checkpointed per scene, rerun skips finished scenes. *Implemented here; should be the platform default.* |
| 7 | P1 | **Host-safety guard rails for local runs.** Ray sizes itself to the whole machine. | Concurrent CUDA contexts on a laptop GPU crashed the driver twice (bugcheck 0x116). | Default CPU/GPU caps with headroom, a RAM / temperature admission check, and a clear "device" switch. *Implemented here as `recompute.max_cpus`, `min_free_ram_frac`, `max_gpu_temp_c`, `device`.* |
| 8 | P1 | **Box-convention declarations.** Model output conventions (yaw zero direction, w/l order, bottom vs centre z) are tribal knowledge. | The KITTI checkpoint uses mmdet3d-v0 boxes (`yaw_std = -θ - π/2`, z at bottom). I proved it empirically against matched GT. | A schema field per prediction source declaring frame, yaw convention and z reference, validated on ingest against a few matched GT. |
| 9 | P1 | **"Why" on every FP/FN, plus map context.** Raw TP/FP/FN counts don't tell a model owner what to fix. | v2's vehicle precision drop looked like a model failure. It was only explained by splitting FPs by reason (`background`) and then checking them against the map's drivable area: most were parked cars off-road that AV2 doesn't label. | Reason codes are part of the eval output and filterable in the viewer (implemented here in `matches.parquet.reason`). |
| 10 | P2 | **Entity lifetime ergonomics for per-frame boxes.** In Foxglove `SceneEntity`, boxes persist forever unless deleted or given a lifetime. | Per the schema, boxes of tracks that end would linger, so the exporter sets a 120 ms lifetime by hand, a guess tied to the 10 Hz sweep rate. | A documented "replace per message" mode for per-frame detections. |
| 11 | P2 | **Viewer access without sign-in for local files.** The Foxglove app requires an account even for a local MCAP. | Blocked an automated screen recording here; the demo video is rendered from pipeline outputs instead. | Local-file open works signed-out, or a headless render CLI exists. This repo ships `replay render` as the fallback. |
| 12 | P2 | **Dataset-native frame IDs.** AV2 annotations join to sweeps only by `timestamp_ns`, and camera frames are at a different rate. | Needed a nearest-timestamp join (`camera_dt_ms` column) and an integrity check that every annotation has a sweep. | Ingest publishes a canonical `frame_id` and the sensor-sync residuals as a QA table. |
| 13 | P1 | **Evaluation-policy parity checks.** Nothing warned that our evaluator diverged from the dataset's official policy (AV2 ROI filtering). | Produced a false 20-point precision regression (docs/DISCREPANCY.md §1). | Eval configs declare which official protocol they mirror, and a CI check compares against the reference implementation on a fixture log. |
| 14 | P2 | **Range-of-validity overlay.** Nothing in the viewer shows the model's input crop. | v1 "missing" every object behind the ego looked like a model failure until I computed which GT fell inside its 0–69 m forward crop (`outside_model_input_crop`). | Viewer can draw a model's declared input ROI and grey out GT outside it. |
